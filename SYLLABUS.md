# Headset Academy: 26-week plan (draft for approval)

Pace: about 90 min/day, 5 days/week. Every week ends with a checkpoint (quiz + 2 scored calls). Every 4th week is a mock QA review. Spanish runs in parallel from week 3 (2 of the 5 days).

Every issue is taught the same way (6-10 lessons each):
1. Concept: what it is, why it breaks (plain words)
2. Diagnose: isolation questions, decision tree
3. Fix: step-by-step with exact words to say
4. Conversation: friendly / neutral / angry customer versions
5. Hard variant: something goes wrong, fix doesn't work, escalate
6. Notes + QA: ticket note, what QA scores, common mistakes
7. Call ladder: Easy, Normal, Hard, Mixed (2 problems in one call)

## Phase 1: Foundations (weeks 1-3)
- W1 Call flow, voice and tone, empathy, active listening, positive language
- W2 De-escalation, hold/transfer, ticket notes, call control, objection handling
- W3 KPIs (AHT, FCR, CSAT), compliance (PCI, HIPAA, privacy, ID verification), knowledge base use, typing and multitasking drills

## Phase 2: TSR L1 (weeks 4-9)
- W4 Troubleshooting method; connectivity basics (lights, cables, power cycle)
- W5 Wi-Fi basics, speed tests, router setup, device pairing
- W6 Windows: slow PC, updates, passwords/lockouts, printers
- W7 Mac, iPhone/Android: settings, backups, app issues
- W8 Email and accounts: Outlook/Gmail setup, MFA, phishing basics
- W9 Software and peripherals: installs, browsers, VPN basics, POS/terminals; L1 exam

## Phase 3: TSR L2 (weeks 10-15)
- W10 DNS, DHCP, IP, ipconfig/ping/tracert/nslookup
- W11 Intermittent Wi-Fi: channels, interference, mesh, band steering
- W12 Slow/dropping connections: packet loss, MTU, duplex, ISP-side vs home
- W13 Outlook profiles, sync, Exchange/M365, DNS for email
- W14 Remote support: screen sharing, event logs, safe mode, drivers
- W15 Business networks: VLANs, firewalls, static IPs, VPN; L2 exam

## Phase 4: TSR L3 and incidents (weeks 16-18)
- W16 Outage triage: single customer vs area; reading logs and signal levels
- W17 Escalation tickets engineering acts on; root cause; problem management
- W18 Incident communication for business customers; SLAs; post-incident review; L3 exam

## Phase 5: CSR, all accounts (weeks 19-22)
- W19 Telecom billing: bill shock, promos, credits, payments, moves
- W20 Retail: orders, shipping, returns, refunds, damaged items
- W21 Banking: fraud, disputes, card lock, account access (strict verification)
- W22 Health insurance: claims, denials, benefits, appeals; retention/cancellations

## Phase 6: Bilingual Spanish (weeks 3-24, parallel)
- Greeting, verification, closing (usted); numbers, dates, names, spelling
- Tech vocabulary: L1, L2, L3 scripts in Spanish
- Billing, bank, retail, insurance vocabulary
- Empathy and de-escalation in Spanish; code-switching; accent/pronunciation drills
- Spanish checkpoint calls every 2 weeks; final Spanish mock

## Phase 7: Readiness (weeks 23-26)
- W23 Mixed-account calls, random scenarios, time pressure
- W24 Mock QA evaluation (full 9-criterion form), review and fix weak criteria
- W25 Final exam: written + 6 scored calls (2 tech, 2 CSR, 2 Spanish)
- W26 Interview prep, resume talking points, "ready for the floor" report

## Pass rules
- Quiz 80% / call 85% on the QA scorecard to unlock the next week
- Missed items return as flashcards (spaced repetition)
- Progress is saved on your device; export/import to back up
